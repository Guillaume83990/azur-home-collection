The best restaurants of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez — 2026 selection

Starred restaurants, hidden trattorias, gastronomic beaches — our selection of essential addresses to complement an exceptional stay in the Var.

Gastronomy: the sixth sense of a Provençal stay

A stay in the Gulf of Saint-Tropez cannot be improvised at the table. The best addresses are fully booked four to six weeks in advance in high season, and starred restaurants are sometimes reserved as early as the preceding winter. Our concierge service handles these reservations for you — but here is our editorial selection of addresses worth the effort.

The unmissable gastronomic establishments

La Vague d'Or (Résidence de la Pinède, Saint-Tropez) — Three Michelin stars, chef Arnaud Donckele. The absolute reference table of the Gulf, with a breathtaking view over the bay. Tasting menu only, minimum €350 per person. Book 2 months in advance in season.

L'Olivier (Hôtel Byblos) — Provençal cuisine revisited by a chef trained in the finest Parisian houses. The terrace shaded by plane trees is one of the most beautiful in Saint-Tropez. Excellent value for a restaurant of this calibre.

Le Girelier (Quai Jean Jaurès) — The reference address for fish and seafood. Bouillabaisse, salt-crusted sea bream, sea urchins in season — here, freshness is guaranteed by the port's fish market, accessible on foot. Terrace looking directly onto the harbour yachts.

Gastronomic beaches

Saint-Tropez invented the concept of the gastronomic beach restaurant, and the best Pampelonne establishments distinguish themselves as much by their cuisine as their service.

Club 55 (Ramatuelle) — The institution. Founded in 1955 during the filming of "And God Created Woman", Club 55 is also a serious gastronomic address, with a crudité of garden vegetables that defies any starred kitchen. Reservation essential, waiting list for new tables.

Nikki Beach (Ramatuelle) — For a festive lunch and impeccable service. The cuisine is refined, the cocktails excellent and the atmosphere international. Sunday lunch is an institution in summer.

Tahiti Plage (Ramatuelle) — The beach closest to the original spirit of Pampelonne. Less frequented than its neighbours, honest Mediterranean cuisine, attentive service. Ideal for families with children.

Hidden addresses

La Ponche (Old Saint-Tropez) — A hotel-restaurant nestled in the lanes of the old town, far from tourist flows. The dining room opens onto a secret terrace. Seasonal menu, short card, impeccable quality.

Lou Cagnard (Grimaud) — Twenty minutes from Saint-Tropez, this village inn offers an authentic Provençal experience at infinitely more reasonable prices than seaside venues. For connoisseurs only.

Les Moulins de Ramatuelle — Perched above the village, this panoramic restaurant offers the most beautiful sunset view of the peninsula. Ideal for a romantic dinner in September.
