What is the best time to rent a villa in Saint-Tropez?

High season, shoulder or winter — each period offers a radically different experience in the Gulf. Our complete analysis to optimise your stay.

July–August: the mythical high season

July and August concentrate the bulk of activity on the peninsula. Pampelonne beaches are packed by 11am, restaurants are fully booked two weeks in advance and seafront roads become giant car parks by late afternoon. Yet this period remains the most sought-after — and for good reason: the light is incomparable, the water reaches 25°C and the nocturnal effervescence is unique in Europe.

For whom? Travellers who want to experience Saint-Tropez at its most intense, who have planned well ahead (the best villas are booked 12 to 18 months in advance) and for whom budget is not the primary constraint.

Rates: expect a multiplier of 2 to 3 compared to shoulder months. A villa at €15,000/week in May can reach €35,000 to €45,000/week in August.

June and September: the perfect months

June and September are, in our view, the two most enjoyable months for a luxury stay in the Gulf. The weather is ideal — between 24 and 28°C — the sea is perfectly warm, restaurants are accessible and beaches breathe. September's light, more golden and less harsh than midsummer, transforms the landscapes into paintings.

For whom? Families with children (outside school holidays), couples seeking tranquillity, gastronomic travellers who want to book the best tables without waiting.

Rates: generally 30 to 50% lower than July-August, with the same quality of service and last-minute villa availability.

May and October: understated luxury

These two months frame the high season and offer a distinctly more intimate experience. In May, the vegetation is lush and mimosa blossoms still perfume the air. In October, the oblique sunlight creates exceptional photographic atmospheres and the sea remains warm (22°C in early month).

For whom? Nature lovers, photographers, couples without school calendar constraints, owners visiting their villa between lettings.

November to April: the Gulf off-season

The Gulf of Saint-Tropez off-season is a well-kept secret. The villages regain their authentic Provençal character, markets overflow with local produce and many gastronomic restaurants reopen in November after the summer closure. Our villas with heated pools remain fully usable until November and from March.

For whom? Remote workers, retirees, creatives in residence, families extending Christmas holidays in an exceptional setting.

Rates: 60 to 70% lower than high season, with maximum flexibility on arrival and departure dates.

Our recommendation by profile

  • Family with young children: June or early September — calm waters, less crowded beaches, ideal temperatures.
  • Group of friends: late June or July — maximum atmosphere, events, Pampelonne beaches.
  • Romantic couple: September or May — intimacy, gastronomy, exceptional light.
  • Work-holiday stay: October to April — absolute calm, optimal internet connection, preferential rates.
