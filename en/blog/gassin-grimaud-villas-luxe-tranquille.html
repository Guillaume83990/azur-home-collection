Gassin & Grimaud: the Gulf's secret destinations for a crowd-free stay

Just a few kilometres from Saint-Tropez, Gassin and Grimaud offer a more intimate luxury experience, breathtaking panoramas and villas where silence is included.

Gassin: the village that overlooks the entire Gulf

Listed among the most beautiful villages in France, Gassin is perched 200 metres above sea level on a rocky spur from which, on a clear day, the eye takes in the Îles d'Or, Cap Lardier and the Alpes-Maritimes. This exceptional panoramic position makes it the preferred destination of architects and interior designers who conceive villas where the view is an integral part of the project.

The villas in the heights of Gassin offer 180° or 270° panoramas that have no equivalent in the Gulf — not even in Saint-Tropez. And paradoxically, despite this obvious beauty, Gassin remains significantly less frequented than its neighbours.

Our villas in Gassin: Villa Horizon (Gulf panorama, 12 guests), Villa Pampelonne (Les Hauts, 14 guests), Villa Azurine (Croix de Fer, 6 guests), Villa Sainte-Maxe (Route des Salins, 16 guests), Palais de la Méditerranée (Pointe des Issambres, 18 guests).

Grimaud: medieval authenticity

In contrast to the worldly atmosphere of Saint-Tropez, Grimaud offers a Provençal experience in its most authentic form. Its ruined medieval castle dominates a golden stone village where it is pleasant to stroll in the morning, before the afternoon heat invites retreat to your villa's pool. The Grimaud market is one of the finest in the Var, with local producers who have never yielded to the sirens of mass tourism.

Port Grimaud, the "Provençal Venice" built in the 1960s by architect François Spoerry, offers a unique experience: colourful houses along navigable canals, accessible directly by boat from the sea.

Our villas in Grimaud: Mas des Oliviers (century-old olive trees, 8 guests), Bastide Saint-Clément (Village, 10 guests), Villa Port Grimaud (Port Grimaud, 12 guests), Domaine de la Garde (Les Maures, 10 guests).

Why choose Gassin or Grimaud over Saint-Tropez?

The question frequently arises from clients who hesitate between the prestige of the name "Saint-Tropez" and the calm of neighbouring communes. Here is our honest answer:

  • The road: from Gassin or Grimaud, Saint-Tropez is 15–20 minutes away outside peak hours. In summer, the ferry from Port Grimaud is even faster and more enjoyable than the road.
  • The price: at equivalent quality, villas in Gassin and Grimaud are on average 15 to 25% cheaper than those in Saint-Tropez proper.
  • The tranquillity: no nightclub revellers at 4am, no yacht engine noise, no queues of cars outside your gate.
  • The scenery: the panoramas from the heights of Gassin are, objectively, among the most beautiful on the entire Côte d'Azur.

What our clients say

Clients who have stayed in Gassin or Grimaud and spent a day in Saint-Tropez almost always return with the same conclusion: "We loved Saint-Tropez for a day, but we're glad to come back to our villa in the evening." This sentence perfectly sums up the balance these two communes offer — the best of both worlds.
