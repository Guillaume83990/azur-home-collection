Which luxury villa to choose in Saint-Tropez according to your travel style?

Contemporary villa with sea view, Provençal bastide nestled in the pines or authentic mas lost in the olive trees — the Gulf of Saint-Tropez offers unrivalled architectural diversity. Our guide to choosing the property that perfectly matches your wishes.

Three worlds, one peninsula

The Gulf of Saint-Tropez concentrates within a few square kilometres one of the finest collections of luxury villas in Europe. 18th-century bastides, signed contemporary architecture and restored Provençal mas coexist in a landscape where the sea is never far away. But each property tells a different story — and corresponds to a particular travel style.

The contemporary villa: for architecture lovers

These properties, often designed by Parisian or Barcelona architecture firms, favour clean lines, structural glass and noble materials — polished concrete, Carrara marble, driftwood. Their trump card: 180° or 270° views over the Mediterranean, infinity pools that seem to merge with the horizon and open living spaces that abolish the boundary between indoors and out.

Ideal for: couples and families who want a design experience, photography enthusiasts or stays where aesthetics matter as much as comfort.

Our selection: Villa Aqua Serena (Saint-Tropez, 14 guests), Villa Horizon (Gassin, 12 guests), Palais de la Méditerranée (Gassin, 18 guests).

The Provençal bastide: for travellers seeking soul

Period terracotta tiles, exposed chestnut beams, Burgundy stone fireplaces and formal French gardens — the Provençal bastide is the complete opposite of contemporary architecture. Carefully restored, it offers a rare authenticity in a world where everything looks the same. These properties, often surrounded by one to three hectare parks, breathe a serenity that photographs cannot convey.

Ideal for: large families, groups of friends, total disconnection weeks or extended stays of two to three weeks.

Our selection: Bastide des Pins (Ramatuelle, 10 guests), Bastide Saint-Clément (Grimaud, 10 guests), Bastide des Roses (Saint-Tropez, 8 guests).

The Provençal mas: for authenticity seekers

More intimate than the bastide, the mas is the oldest architectural form in the Var. Built over generations, it is recognisable by its thick walls that keep cool in summer, its painted wooden shutters and its terraces shaded by old mulberry trees or century-old olive trees. Our mas have all been renovated by master craftsmen who preserved every historical detail while discreetly integrating contemporary comfort.

Ideal for: couples, small families or immersive stays in Provençal culture.

Our selection: Mas des Oliviers (Grimaud, 8 guests), Mas du Coucher de Soleil (Ramatuelle, 8 guests).

The criteria that truly make a difference

Beyond architectural style, three criteria determine the experience:

  • The view: panoramic sea, gulf, pine forest or gardens — each orientation creates a different atmosphere. Properties on elevated ground (Gassin, Les Hauts de Ramatuelle) offer the most beautiful panoramas; those near the seafront road offer direct beach access.
  • Capacity: our villas accommodate 6 to 20 guests. For groups of more than 12, we recommend a property with multiple living spaces and at least two dining rooms.
  • The pool: infinity, mirror, natural with plants or seawater — the type of pool significantly influences the property's atmosphere.

Our advice: book beyond the photograph

Photos of luxury villas are always beautiful. What makes the difference is what the photo doesn't show: sun exposure, actual distance to the beach, noise from a nearby road or the quality of the cleaning and concierge teams. That's why we favour a phone call with each client before any booking — to precisely understand your project and guide you towards the property that truly suits you.
