Property management in Saint-Tropez: how to maximise your income in 2026?

Dynamic pricing, premium platforms, tenant selection — the strategies of owners who achieve +30% revenue compared to self-management.

The luxury rental market in Saint-Tropez in 2026

The luxury villa market in the Gulf of Saint-Tropez has never been more dynamic. International demand — British, Scandinavian, American and Middle Eastern clientele — remains structurally higher than supply during July and August weeks. For owners with a quality property and professional management, revenues can reach exceptional levels.

According to our 2025 data, villas managed by Azur Villa Prestige generated on average 38% more revenue than comparable properties managed independently. The difference is explained by three main levers.

Lever 1: dynamic pricing

Setting a single rate for the entire season is the most costly mistake a luxury villa owner can make. The market is extraordinarily volatile: a villa can rent for €8,000/week in May and €42,000/week for the week of 14 July. Our pricing algorithm analyses in real time the competition, local events (Monaco Grand Prix, Cannes Film Festival, Voile de Saint-Tropez), occupancy rate and current enquiries to adjust prices every week.

Result: we maximise revenue per week on premium slots while avoiding low periods through attractive shoulder season rates.

Lever 2: multi-channel distribution

Mass-market platforms like Airbnb or Booking are not suited to the luxury villa market — their commission (20 to 25%), clientele and positioning do not match the expectations of a €15,000/week property. We distribute your properties via:

  • Our website and direct network: 60% of bookings, zero commission for you, qualified clientele.
  • Luxury specialist portals: VRBO Premium, Luxury Retreats, villanovo, Villas & More — qualified international audience.
  • Agent network: luxury travel agencies in the UK, Scandinavia, USA and Middle East with whom we have worked for over 5 years.

Lever 3: quality of presentation

A luxury villa is first sold through images. We systematically invest in professional photography (specialist architectural photographer, drone, 3D virtual tour) and presentation texts written by copywriters specialising in luxury tourism — in French, English, German and Italian. An impeccable presentation listing can double the conversion rate of visitors into bookings.

Tenant selection: protecting your property

The profitability of a luxury villa is not measured only by gross revenue — it is also measured by costs avoided. A disrespectful tenant can cost €15,000 to €50,000 in damage, repairs and weeks lost to renovation. Our selection process includes: identity verification, systematic security deposit (10 to 20% of rent), prior telephone interview and behavioural scoring based on our history of 500+ lettings.

Key figures for a 500 m² villa in Saint-Tropez

  • Estimated annual gross revenues: €180,000 to €320,000 depending on location and amenities
  • Target occupancy rate: 18 to 22 weeks/year (June–September + a few off-season weeks)
  • Azur Villa Prestige management commission: 20% of net revenues
  • Annual operating costs: €15,000 to €35,000 (maintenance, cleaning, minor repairs)
