Private chef in Saint-Tropez: how to compose the perfect dinner in your villa?

Bespoke gastronomic menu, wine pairings, table setting — our chef guides you to transform your villa into a starred restaurant for an evening.

The private chef experience: far more than a meal

Hiring a private chef for your Saint-Tropez villa isn't simply delegating the cooking — it's transforming your terrace or dining room into a personalised gastronomic stage. Our partner chefs work exclusively on a bespoke basis: every menu is conceived after an in-depth interview with you, taking into account your preferences, any allergies, the number of guests and the occasion being celebrated.

Choosing your culinary style

The Gulf of Saint-Tropez is a land of gastronomic contrasts, and our chefs reflect this diversity:

  • Contemporary Mediterranean: local produce (fish from the Saint-Tropez fish market, vegetables from the La Garde-Freinet market), light and colourful preparations, Italian and Provençal inspiration.
  • French gastronomic: revisited classic techniques, worked sauces, sophisticated food-wine pairings with bottles from your villa's wine cellar.
  • World food: for cosmopolitan groups, our chefs master cuisines from around the world — Japanese, Lebanese, Peruvian — prepared with the finest local produce.
  • Luxury barbecue: a summer classic. Whole fish, aged prime rib, wood-grilled vegetables — our chef transforms the barbecue into a memorable gastronomic experience.

The staging: the art of setting the table

A private chef's service includes, on request, complete table staging: crockery selected from our partner inventory, fresh flowers from the local nursery, candle or fairy light illumination in the olive trees. Our table stylists work in coordination with the chef to ensure the table's aesthetic harmonises with the menu and desired atmosphere.

Wine pairings: a sommelier at your service

For gala dinners or special occasions, we offer the services of an independent sommelier who selects wines to match each course. Their knowledge of Var wine estates — AOC Côtes de Provence, Bandol, Palette — is invaluable. They can also organise a private tasting in a partner estate's cellar on the afternoon before dinner.

Practical organisation: it all begins two days before

Our process is simple and structured:

  1. D-2: 20-minute call with our concierge to define the dinner concept, number of covers, dietary constraints and budget.
  2. D-1: the chef confirms the final menu, shops at the Saint-Tropez market or directly from our partner suppliers (fishermen, cheesemongers, market gardeners).
  3. Day of: the chef arrives 2 hours before service to prepare in the kitchen, sets the table 45 minutes before and welcomes you as in a restaurant.
  4. After service: full washing-up and cleaning included — you'll find no trace of their visit, only the memory of an exceptional meal.

Indicative pricing

Rates vary according to the chef, number of covers and menu complexity. Budget approximately €150 to €350 per person for a complete 5 to 7-course dinner, excluding wines. Our concierge packages often incorporate two or three chef evenings into a week's rental — an option very popular with families who want to alternate between restaurants and dining at home.
