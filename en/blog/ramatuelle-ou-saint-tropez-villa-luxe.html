Ramatuelle or Saint-Tropez: which address for your luxury villa?

Mythical peninsula or wild intimacy — these two destinations do not offer the same experience. Our comparison to help you choose wisely.

Saint-Tropez: the global icon

Saint-Tropez isn't a destination — it's a myth. Brigitte Bardot, Picasso, Les Caves du Roy, the harbour with its giant yachts, the cobbled streets of the old town where every boutique is an invitation to luxury... Renting a villa in Saint-Tropez means writing yourself into a story that transcends the mere seaside stay.

Advantages: everything is within walking distance or water taxi — starred restaurants, nightclubs, art galleries, the Tuesday and Saturday Provençal market. The atmosphere is constant from early June to mid-September.

Disadvantages: roads are saturated in July-August, restaurant and shop prices are at their peak, and nightlife can be disruptive if you're seeking tranquillity.

Our villas in Saint-Tropez: Villa Aqua Serena (Canebiers), Villa Émeraude (Cap Tahiti), Villa Les Parcs, Le Château Blanc (La Citadelle), Bastide des Roses (La Bouillabaisse), Villa Tahiti Beach.

Ramatuelle: the connoisseur's secret

Ten kilometres from Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle is everything the peninsula has that is most authentic. Its hilltop village, its medieval lanes, its summer theatre festival and its Pampelonne beaches accessible on foot from some villas make it a serious alternative for travellers who know what they're looking for.

Advantages: relative calm even in high season, spectacular landscapes of the Hauts de Ramatuelle, direct access to Pampelonne beaches (Tahiti, Club 55, Nikki Beach), more accessible gastronomy, easier parking.

Disadvantages: no lively village life in the evenings, getting to Saint-Tropez requires a car or water taxi, fewer luxury shops nearby.

Our villas in Ramatuelle: Bastide des Pins (L'Escalet), Domaine de la Pinède (Les Parcs), Villa Pampelonne (Les Hauts), Mas du Coucher de Soleil (Bonne Terrasse), Villa Cap Camarat, Villa L'Oliveraie (La Croix Valmer).

Our comparison in figures

  • Distance to Pampelonne beaches: Saint-Tropez 8 km / Ramatuelle 0–3 km
  • Distance to Saint-Tropez harbour: Saint-Tropez 0–3 km / Ramatuelle 8–12 km
  • Noise level in high season: Saint-Tropez high / Ramatuelle moderate
  • Villa rates: Saint-Tropez +15% on average / Ramatuelle reference
  • Provençal authenticity: Saint-Tropez low / Ramatuelle high

Our verdict

If you're visiting the Gulf for the first time, for a milestone anniversary or a short intense stay, choose Saint-Tropez. If you're returning for the second or third time, if you have children who want the beach every day, or if you're looking for a balance between luxury and authenticity, Ramatuelle is the answer.

In any case, the distance between the two is so short that by renting a villa in one, you easily access the other.
