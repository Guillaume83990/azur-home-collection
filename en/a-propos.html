What guides us

Our values

01

Selectivity

We refuse expansion for expansion's sake. Every property we accept must meet our strict standards — architecture, location, facilities and hosting potential.

02

Local presence

We are physically present in the Gulf of Saint-Tropez. Not a platform, not a call centre. A local team that knows every property and can be there in 15 minutes.

03

Total transparency

With our owners: detailed monthly report, real-time portal access, bank transfers within 48h. With our tenants: no unpleasant surprises, everything is precisely what we promise.

04

Absolute discretion

Our clients — owners and tenants alike — include some of the most high-profile individuals. Confidentiality is not an option; it is a founding commitment of Azur Villa Prestige.
