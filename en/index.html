Contemporary Architecture Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Canebiers

Villa Aqua Serena

A pure dialogue between raw concrete, glass and the Mediterranean. Designed by a Parisian architecture firm, this villa unfolds 580 m² across three terraced levels. Its 22-metre infinity pool erases the horizon — the waters of Saint-Tropez Bay seem to begin at your feet. Inside, refined spaces, a Bulthaup kitchen open to the sea and a private spa carved in natural stone.

Provençal Bastide Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Ramatuelle

Bastide des Pins

Two hectares of wild Provence guarded by a forest of century-old pines. This 18th-century bastide, restored by a Milanese interior designer, marries the golden stones of the region with the finest materials — natural linen, bleached oak, artisanal tiles. The private estate reveals a permaculture kitchen garden, a clay tennis court and a natural pool with emerald reflections.

🏊 Natural pool 🌳 Private estate 2 ha 🎾 Tennis court 420 m² 10 guests

Haute Couture Design Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Gassin

Villa Horizon

Suspended above the gulf, the five suites of Villa Horizon each open onto a unique panorama between Sainte-Maxime and the Golden Islands. The designer — former artistic director of a Parisian palace hotel — conceived each room as a living painting: saddle leather, Carrara marble and brushed brass dialogue with the 18-metre mirror pool. A Technogym gym, home cinema and helipad complete the ensemble.

🏊 Mirror pool 18m 🌊 180° gulf view 🏋️ Technogym gym 650 m² 12 guests

Provençal Mas Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Grimaud

Mas des Oliviers

Hidden behind a curtain of three-hundred-year-old olive trees, this 17th-century Provençal mas exudes a serenity that time seems not to have touched. Its renovation — entrusted to a master craftsman — preserved every exposed beam, terracotta tile and barrel vault. The turquoise mosaic pool, the aromatic kitchen garden and the wisteria-shaded terrace compose a Provençal art de vivre elevated to its most absolute level of excellence.

🏊 Mosaic pool 🌿 Park 1.5 ha 🍷 Private wine cellar 380 m² 8 guests
