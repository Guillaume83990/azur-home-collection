Villa Aqua Serena

A pure dialogue between raw concrete, glass and the Mediterranean. Designed by a Parisian architectural firm, this villa unfolds 580 m² across three terraced levels. Its 22-metre infinity pool erases the horizon — the waters of the Bay of Saint-Tropez seem to begin at your feet. Inside, pared-back volumes, a Bulthaup kitchen open to the sea and a private spa carved from natural stone.

Bastide des Pins

Two hectares of wild Provence sheltered by a forest of centennial pines. This 18th-century bastide, fully restored by a Milanese interior designer, marries the region's golden stone with the finest materials — natural linen, bleached oak, artisan ceramics. The private estate reveals a permaculture kitchen garden, a clay tennis court and a natural pool with emerald reflections.

Haute couture design Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Gassin

Villa Horizon

Suspended above the gulf, Villa Horizon's five suites each open onto a unique panorama stretching from Sainte-Maxime to the Golden Islands. The designer — former creative director of a Parisian palace — conceived every room as a living tableau: saddle leather, Carrara marble and brushed brass in dialogue with the 18-metre mirror pool. A Technogym fitness room, home cinema and helipad complete the ensemble.

Provençal mas Gulf of Saint-Tropez · Grimaud

Mas des Oliviers

Hidden behind a curtain of three-hundred-year-old olive trees, this 17th-century Provençal mas exudes a serenity that time seems not to have touched. Its restoration — entrusted to a master craftsman — has preserved every exposed beam, every terracotta tile and every barrel vault. The turquoise mosaic pool, the aromatic kitchen garden and the wisteria-shaded terrace compose a Provençal art de vivre elevated to its most absolute excellence.
